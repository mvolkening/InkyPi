Reads PowerSum, PowerA, PowerB, VoltageA, VoltageB, Frequency, and PowerFactorA/B from an InfluxDB v2 bucket (e.g. a whole-home energy monitor with two split-phase legs) and shows the current draw, 6h/24h history, a 30-day daily-energy view, and live Voltage/Frequency/Power Factor gauges. If your meter also reports a cumulative Total Energy counter, it's used to make daily and Time-of-Use energy/cost figures more accurate. Store your InfluxDB API token in the .env file as INFLUXDB_TOKEN (see the "Requires API Key" note above).

Time-of-Use pricing follows Ontario's fixed schedule: weekends and Ontario's ~9 statutory holidays (New Year's, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas, Boxing Day) are Off-Peak all day - calculated automatically every year, including moving dates like Good Friday, so nothing needs to be maintained here. Weekdays follow a Summer (May 1 - Oct 31) or Winter (Nov 1 - Apr 30) pattern: Summer is Mid-Peak 7-11am, On-Peak 11am-5pm, Mid-Peak 5-7pm, Off-Peak overnight; Winter is On-Peak 7-11am, Mid-Peak 11am-5pm, On-Peak 5-7pm, Off-Peak overnight. Only the three rates below need updating when your utility changes its pricing (typically each November 1st and May 1st) - the schedule itself is fixed by the Ontario Energy Board and doesn't change.
